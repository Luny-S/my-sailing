\section*{2. Emergency Procedures / Procedury alarmowe}

\subsection*{Man Overboard (MOB)}
\begin{enumerate}[noitemsep]
    \item Shout \textbf{"MAN OVERBOARD!"} and press MOB on GPS/plotter.
    \item Throw life ring / MOB buoy immediately.
    \item Assign a lookout — keep eyes on the person at all times.
    \item Skipper takes the helm. Call remaining crew.
    \item Approach manoeuvre (Quick Stop or Reach \& Return).
    \item VHF Ch~16 MAYDAY if the situation is critical.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Fire / Pożar}
\begin{enumerate}[noitemsep]
    \item Shout \textbf{"FIRE!"} — shut off gas and fuel immediately.
    \item Use extinguisher. Do not open hatch before the fire is out.
    \item If uncontrolled: issue MAYDAY, prepare to abandon ship.
    \item Muster point: \field{5cm}
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Flooding / Zalanie}
\begin{enumerate}[noitemsep]
    \item Locate source, close sea cocks.
    \item Activate bilge pump: electric \field{3cm} / manual \field{3cm}.
    \item Stuff rags or soft wood plugs into the breach.
    \item If sinking: MAYDAY, deploy life raft.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{4. Mediterranean-specific Hazards / Specyfika Morza Śródziemnego}

\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
    \item \textbf{Mistral / Bora / Meltemi} — can escalate rapidly; check meteo every morning.
    \item Heavy ferry and commercial traffic — maintain radar/AIS watch.
    \item Rocks and reefs in Croatian/Greek waters — use official charts, not just apps.
    \item Night sailing: use tethers at all times on deck.
    \item Customs (clearance-in) required when entering non-EU waters.
    \item Sun, heat, dehydration — hydration schedule on long passages.
\end{itemize}

    \item Sea cock locations: \field{6cm}
    \item Report any abnormality to the skipper immediately.
\end{itemize}
